# Escalation and SLA

## Response targets by urgency
Critical tickets (safety hazards, electrical smoke, leaking appliances, or active payment fraud) get a human specialist callback within 15 minutes. High-urgency tickets receive a response within 2 hours, medium within 8 business hours, and low within 24 business hours.

## Human handoff policy
Customers can request a human specialist at any point in the conversation. Conversations with sustained customer frustration or repeated order-lookup failures transfer automatically with all collected details preserved.
