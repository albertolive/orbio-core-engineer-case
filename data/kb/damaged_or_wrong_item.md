# Damaged or Wrong Items

## Damaged item replacement SLA
When an item arrives cracked, leaking, or broken, NovaMart ships a free express replacement within 48 hours of ticket creation. Customers do not need to wait for the damaged unit to reach the warehouse before the replacement ships.

## Wrong item received
If the warehouse sent the wrong product or wrong size, NovaMart dispatches the correct item via 24-hour express courier as soon as the support ticket is confirmed and emails a free QR drop-off code for the wrong item.

## Instant goodwill credit
Support specialists can apply up to EUR 25 in instant account credit on high-urgency damaged or wrong-item orders without supervisor approval.
