# Returns and Refunds

## Return window
Customers can return most physical items within 30 calendar days of delivery for a full refund. Items returned between day 31 and day 45 qualify for NovaMart store credit only. Digital memberships such as NovaCare can be canceled within 14 days if no benefits were redeemed.

## Refund timing
Once the carrier scans the prepaid return label, NovaMart issues refunds to the original payment method within 3 to 5 business days. Store credit refunds post immediately after the carrier drop-off scan.

## Return shipping fees
Returns for damaged, defective, or wrong items include a free prepaid return label. Preference returns (such as changing size or color) have a flat EUR 4.90 return label fee deducted from the refund.
