# Shipping and Delays

## Standard and express delivery times
Standard shipping takes 3 to 5 business days within Spain and the EU. Express shipping delivers in 24 to 48 hours on business days when ordered before 16:00 CET.

## Delayed packages in transit
If a package shows no tracking movement for 72 hours or misses its promised delivery date by more than 2 business days, NovaMart refunds any express shipping fee and opens a carrier trace. If the parcel is not delivered within 7 calendar days of the original estimate, NovaMart dispatches a free replacement or full refund.

## Missing delivered packages
When tracking marks a parcel as delivered but the customer has not received it, support asks the customer to check with neighbors or building reception first, and files a lost-parcel replacement claim within 24 hours if still missing.
